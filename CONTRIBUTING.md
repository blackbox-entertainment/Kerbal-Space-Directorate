# Contributing to Kerbal Space Directorate

Thanks for your interest. **This project does not accept code contributions.** Pull requests will be closed without review. You are welcome to fork it under the [MIT License](LICENSE).

## Bug reports and feedback are welcome

Open an issue with the **Bug report** template. The most useful things to include:

- What you did, what you expected, and what happened.
- Your `KSP.log` (in the KSP folder). Every line from this mod starts with `[KSA]`.
- Your KSP version and mod list (CKAN: File > Export modlist).
- Whether AI features were on, and which model. **Never paste your API key.**

Questions and ideas can go in an issue too.

## If this ever changes

Should Blackbox Entertainment open the project to contributions later, contributors will be asked to sign off their commits (`git commit -s`, the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/)) and to agree that Blackbox may relicense their work.
